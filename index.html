03 / A useful method

Turn Litbuy finds
into a shortlist.

A long list is only useful when its entries are comparable. This Litbuy shopping guide uses four simple checks to help you decide which links deserve a second look.

Start your product research ↗

Choose a clear purpose.

Separate daily sneakers from statement footwear, lightweight layers from winter clothing, and practical accessories from decorative pieces. Add a short “why I saved this” note so similar-looking items do not become interchangeable.

Capture the exact variant.

Save the original product link together with the color, size and model shown in the listing. A product page may contain several options with different prices or materials. Record when you checked it so older notes are easier to recognize.

Compare measurable details.

For a Litbuy sneaker spreadsheet, note the sizing system and any model-specific fit information. For clothing, compare garment measurements with an item you own. For jewelry and headwear, check dimensions, adjustment ranges and stated materials.

Review the full buying decision.

Separate the listed item price from delivery, service charges and possible destination costs. Review the seller, return conditions and available evidence. Treat missing information as an unanswered question before committing to a purchase.

Shoes & sneakers Model, colorway, selected size, size system, listed materials and price. Whether the size chart applies to that model and the selected listing variant.
Tops & outerwear Chest, shoulders, garment length, sleeve length, fabric description and fit. Measurement units, flat versus circumference measurements, and any stated tolerance.
Pants & shorts Waist, rise, inseam, leg opening, stretch description and selected size. Whether the waistband measurements are relaxed or stretched.

Budget note: record item price, domestic delivery, service charges, international shipping and applicable taxes separately. Keep unconfirmed charges marked as estimates.

Practical answers about product lists, comparison notes and where this directory takes you.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a product-link list used to organize shopping research. Useful fields include the original listing URL, category, selected variant, measurements, listed price and personal comparison notes. A spreadsheet entry alone does not verify a seller or product.

Where do the category links on this page go?

The category links open the corresponding CAFANS category pages. General browse buttons open www.cafans.com. This page is a guide and directory, not a downloadable spreadsheet or checkout.

How should I compare clothing sizes?

Compare the listing's garment measurements with a similar item you already own. Check chest width, shoulder width, length and sleeve length as applicable, and confirm whether measurements are flat, circumference-based or in different units.

Does a product photo prove authenticity?

No. A photo or spreadsheet entry alone does not establish authenticity. Review the seller, provenance, listing details and applicable authentication evidence independently before making a purchase.

What belongs in a shopping budget?

Record the item price, domestic delivery, service charges, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties separately. Use estimates until the relevant provider confirms each charge.

Is this an official Litbuy or brand website?

No. This is an independent shopping research guide directing visitors to CAFANS. It is not an official website of Litbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès.
